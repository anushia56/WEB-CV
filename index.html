about me

Designing calm systems for a fast-moving world.

I turn caffeine and curiosity into digital experiences. Based in Bhutan, I live at the intersection of design and code, the place where a thing doesn't just work, it feels right. I studied Computer Science at Gyalpozhing College of Information Technology, but somewhere between the algorithms and the all-nighters, I fell in love with the craft of building interfaces that people actually enjoy using.

I think in systems and feel in pixels. I obsess over spacing, sweat the small interactions, and genuinely believe that good design is invisible. You only notice it when it's missing. On the engineering side, I build full-stack, frontend to backend, mobile to web, database to deployment.

When I'm not building things, I'm probably sketching the next one, because the idea is always louder than the deadline.
